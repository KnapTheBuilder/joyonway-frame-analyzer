JOYONWAY FRAME ANALYZER

Decode, validate and generate RS-485 frames for the Joyonway 1A/1D family (P23B32, P20B29, P25B85 and compatible). v2.1.0 - CRC algorithm by alexbde.

Credits. Pseudo-escape table and broadcast parser by KDy. CRC-32 algorithm reverse-engineered by alexbde. P20B29 setpoint captures by Yannickt26. P23B32 V2 captures and cross-validation by KnapTheBuilder. Additional captures and ideas by Neuro and old-man.

1. Decode & validate frames

Paste raw hex frames (with or without spaces, one per line or all in one). The analyzer will split, unescape, parse and validate the CRC of each.

2. Generate a setpoint frame (any temperature)

The CRC is computed mathematically (alexbde algorithm), so no capture is required. Range 15.5 C to 40 C, 0.5 C step. Validated by cross-check against Yannickt26's P20B29 wire captures.

3. About the CRC algorithm

Show parameters and validation status
Polynomial0x04C11DB7
Init0x00000000
XorOut0x552D22C8
Reflectionnone, MSB-first
Pre-stepeach 32-bit word of the payload is byte-swapped before CRC
Wire encodinglittle-endian, 4 bytes between payload and 0x1D

The byte-swap step is characteristic of an ARM Cortex-M MCU feeding a hardware CRC peripheral in little-endian word order. This is what makes the algorithm difficult to find with standard CRC analysis tools.

Cross-validated on P23B32 V2 (9 of 11 frames OK) and P20B29 setpoint captures (2 of 2 OK). The 2 failing frames are LIGHT_ON / LIGHT_OFF, which carry a 17-byte payload instead of 16 - likely an extra state byte (RGB mode or brightness) that remains under investigation.
